# ARPA Internet-Draft `-00` Submission Runbook

This runbook operationalizes the IETF Internet-Draft submission process for `draft-sankarshan-agent-registry-protocol-00`.

Authoritative IETF guidance: <https://authors.ietf.org/submitting-your-internet-draft>

## 1. Preconditions

Before opening the Datatracker submission tool:

- use the final repository commit selected for submission;
- confirm `make ietf-check` passes;
- confirm `make release-check-all` passes;
- inspect the generated RFCXML, plaintext, and HTML renderings;
- confirm the document identity is `draft-sankarshan-agent-registry-protocol-00`;
- confirm author metadata is correct;
- confirm the IANA Considerations section still requests no IANA action for `-00`;
- confirm no TODO, FIXME, placeholder, or unresolved submission marker remains.

The RFCXML v3 file is the preferred Datatracker submission artifact. The plaintext rendering may be uploaded alongside it. HTML is retained as a review artifact and is normally generated by Datatracker from RFCXML.

## 2. Account and Datatracker

Create an IETF Datatracker account before submission and sign in. A submission can technically be initiated without signing in, but IETF guidance recommends being logged in because it simplifies author verification.

Submission tool: <https://datatracker.ietf.org/submit/>

## 3. Upload

Upload:

1. `draft-sankarshan-agent-registry-protocol-00.xml` as the authoritative RFCXML v3 Internet-Draft;
2. optionally upload the matching `draft-sankarshan-agent-registry-protocol-00.txt` rendering if the tool offers the companion plaintext field.

Do not upload a ZIP or repository archive.

For this initial individual submission, do not identify another Internet-Draft as being replaced.

## 4. Datatracker validation

Review the submission-tool validation results. Datatracker applies the relevant Internet-Draft tooling, including `xml2rfc`, `id2xml`, and `idnits` as appropriate.

Treat warnings as review items rather than automatically ignoring them. If a warning indicates a material metadata, format, reference, boilerplate, or Internet-Draft convention problem, correct the repository source, regenerate the package, rerun repository validation, and upload the regenerated artifact.

## 5. Metadata review

Before confirming submission, verify at minimum:

- title: `Agent Registry Protocol`;
- draft name/revision: `draft-sankarshan-agent-registry-protocol-00`;
- intended status: Standards Track;
- submission stream: IETF;
- author: Sankarshan Mukhopadhyay;
- affiliation: QBF Consulting LLP;
- email: `sankarshan@qbfconsulting.digital`.

Do not describe the document as an RFC or imply standards status merely because it is submitted as an Internet-Draft.

## 6. Author verification and posting

IETF normally sends the authors an email asking them to verify the submission. The draft is not posted until that verification action is completed.

If the submitter is logged into Datatracker and is recognized as a listed author, Datatracker may bypass the email-verification step.

Complete the verification/posting action immediately after reviewing the submitted metadata and renderings.

## 7. Confirm publication

After posting, verify the Datatracker document page shows revision `-00` and that its generated HTML/plaintext renderings are readable.

Record the Datatracker URL and publication date in this repository.

## 8. Repository closeout

After successful publication:

- update `ietf/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` to mark RFCXML submission, verification, and Datatracker URL recording complete;
- add the Datatracker document URL to `ietf/README.md` and the repository `README.md` where appropriate;
- record the exact submitted commit SHA and a digest of the submitted RFCXML;
- preserve the checked-in `-00` submission snapshot unchanged;
- open the post-submission/community-review tranche for feedback intended for `-01` or later.

Do not silently modify the historical `-00` snapshot after Datatracker publication. Corrections belong in the editable source and a subsequent Internet-Draft revision.

## Failure/recovery path

If Datatracker rejects the upload or exposes a material validation error:

1. capture the exact error/warning;
2. correct the authoritative Markdown/fragments rather than hand-editing generated RFCXML where possible;
3. regenerate XML/TXT/HTML;
4. rerun `make ietf-check` and `make release-check-all`;
5. review the renderings again;
6. retry the Datatracker submission.

If the normal Datatracker tool cannot be used, IETF documents a manual-post path through `support@ietf.org`; use that only as a fallback.
